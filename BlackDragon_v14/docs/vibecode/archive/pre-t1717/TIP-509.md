# TIP-509 — BD-R9: hedge OFF khoa DCA hai chieu (deadlock)

**Wave**: 4 (11/08/2026) · **Muc do**: P3 (nhung hau qua P1 khi trung dieu kien)
**File cham vao**: `Include/BlackDragon/EntryFilters.mqh`, `Include/BlackDragon/Strategy.mqh`
**Test**: `Scripts/BlackDragon/Tests/RunTests.mq5` (+9 assert, tong 37 assert v14.7.2)

## Trieu chung

`Flag_Use_hedge = false`, ca hai chieu cung co vi the → **moi lenh DCA cua CA HAI chieu bi chan vinh vien**. Exit van chay, nen ro bi dong bang o gia trung binh xau nhat va khong con duong gia binh quan.

## Nguyen nhan

`CStrategy::OnTick` muc 3:

```cpp
if(Flag_Use_hedge || m_basket.sell.count == 0) TryGridAdd(ctx, m_basket.buy,  BD_DIR_BUY,  MaxOrdersBuy);
if(Flag_Use_hedge || m_basket.buy.count  == 0) TryGridAdd(ctx, m_basket.sell, BD_DIR_SELL, MaxOrdersSell);
```

Hai dieu kien **loai tru lan nhau**. Khi `buy.count > 0 && sell.count > 0` va hedge OFF thi ca hai deu false. Khong ben nao tu co lai duoc, nen trang thai nay **khong co loi thoat**.

Trang thai hai chieu VAN dat toi duoc du hedge OFF:

1. Nut panel **Open Buy / Open Sell** khong di qua cong hedge (co y: panel nghe nguoi van hanh);
2. `flag_Hand_Ord = true` → lenh tay magic-0 duoc dem vao ca hai chieu qua `Basket_OwnsMagic`.

Dang chu y: cong hedge tren nhanh DCA **mau thuan voi chinh contract ghi trong header `Strategy.mqh`**: "grid adds are gated by pause/news/one-per-bar/MinuteStop only".

## Quyet dinh

Luat hedge la luat cho **SERIES MOI**, khong phai cho DCA. Mot lenh DCA khong the *tao ra* trang thai doi lap: chieu cua no da mo san, chieu kia ton tai bat ke ta lam gi.

## Thay doi

Hai ham thuan trong `EntryFilters.mqh`:

```cpp
bool Hedge_AllowsNewSeries(const bool useHedge, const int oppositeCount)  // luat v13, giu nguyen
bool Hedge_AllowsGridAdd(const int ownCount)                              // KHONG nhan co hedge
```

`Flag_Use_hedge` co y **khong** la tham so cua ham thu hai: nguoi doc chu ky ham thay ngay rang viec thieu phep thu hedge la ban va, khong phai xoa nham.

## Chua lam yeu bao ve

Hedge OFF van chan series moi o chieu doi dien. Assert cuoi cung chot dung dieu do: trong chinh trang thai deadlock, DCA duoc mo khoa **nhung** series moi van bi tu choi.

## Assert (9)

| # | Noi dung |
|---|---|
| 1 | hedge ON: chieu doi dien khong bao gio chan series moi |
| 2 | hedge OFF + doi dien rong: cho phep series moi |
| 3 | hedge OFF + doi dien mo: **chan** series moi (luat v13) |
| 4 | count am (khong the xay ra) coi nhu rong |
| 5 | chieu dang mo duoc them leg |
| 6 | chieu rong khong co gi de them |
| 7 | **Regression**: dung lai cong CU, chung minh no false o CA HAI chieu |
| 8 | cong MOI mo khoa ca hai chieu |
| 9 | DCA duoc mo nhung series doi lap van bi tu choi |

Assert 7 giu cong cu duoi dang **code chay duoc** chu khong phai van xuoi, de khong ai vo tinh dat lai no.

## Blast radius

Mac dinh `Flag_Use_hedge = true` → nhanh nay **no-op** voi cau hinh mac dinh. Chi doi hanh vi khi hedge OFF **va** ton tai vi the hai chieu.
